## Privacy Policy

### 1. Summary

This website is a static personal blog. The author operates no database and does not collect, sell or share personal data directly. Personal data processed in connection with this website is handled by Google (advertising and analytics) and GitHub (hosting and comments), as described below.

### 2. Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses and related technical information for security and operational purposes. The author has no access to these logs. Further information is available in [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

### 3. Advertising

This website displays advertisements served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve advertisements based on a user's prior visits to this website or to other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve advertisements to users based on their visits to this and/or other websites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/). Users may also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/). Information on how Google uses data is available at [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Where required by applicable law, a consent message is displayed before personalised advertising is used. Users may change their choice at any time through the "Privacy and cookie settings" link shown on the page.

### 4. Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to measure traffic and readership. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as pages visited, approximate location, browser and device type. Users may prevent this collection by installing the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

### 5. Comments

Comments are provided through [giscus](https://giscus.app/) and stored as GitHub Discussions. Posting a comment requires a GitHub account and is subject to GitHub's Privacy Statement.

### 6. Your rights and contact

Depending on their jurisdiction, users may have rights under applicable data protection law, including the right to access or request deletion of their personal data. Requests concerning data held by Google or GitHub should be directed to those providers through the links above. Any other enquiry may be sent to the author at the email address shown in the sidebar.

## 隱私權政策

### 摘要

- 本站是靜態個人部落格。本站沒有資料庫，也不販售你的資料。
- Google AdSense 會顯示廣告，並可能使用 cookie。在法律要求的地區，Google 會先徵求你的同意。
- Google Analytics 會統計造訪次數。
- 留言使用 giscus，需要 GitHub 帳號。
- 本站由 GitHub Pages 託管，GitHub 可能記錄你的 IP 位址。

本站（`shihtiy.com`）是個人部落格。本站不需要註冊帳號；除非你主動聯絡我或留言，本站不會收集你的姓名或 email。

### 託管（GitHub Pages）

本站由 [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/) 託管。你造訪本站時，GitHub 可能為了安全與服務運作記錄你的 IP 位址和其他技術資料。我看不到也無法更改這些紀錄。詳見 [GitHub 隱私權聲明](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)。

### 廣告（Google AdSense）

本站顯示由 Google AdSense 提供的廣告。Google 與其合作夥伴會使用 cookie，依據你過去造訪本站或其他網站的紀錄投放廣告。

- 你可以在 [Google 廣告設定](https://adssettings.google.com/) 關閉個人化廣告。
- Google 如何使用資料，請見 [Google 如何使用合作網站或應用程式的資訊](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)。
- 部分第三方廠商也可能經由 Google 投放廣告，可以到 [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/) 選擇退出。

在當地法律要求取得同意的地區，Google 會在使用個人化廣告前先顯示同意訊息。你可以隨時透過該訊息更改選擇。

### 流量分析（Google Analytics）

本站使用 Google Analytics 統計造訪次數與閱讀的頁面。Google Analytics 會使用 cookie，並收集造訪頁面、大略位置、瀏覽器與裝置類型等資料。你可以安裝 [Google Analytics 停用瀏覽器外掛程式](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) 停止追蹤。

### 留言（giscus）

留言功能由 [giscus](https://giscus.app/) 提供，留言會存放在 GitHub Discussions。留言需要 GitHub 帳號，並適用 [GitHub 隱私權聲明](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)。

### 你的權利

依你所在地區的個人資料保護法律，你可能享有查閱與刪除個人資料等權利。Google 或 GitHub 持有的資料，請使用上方連結處理；其他事項請用側邊欄的 email 聯絡我。
