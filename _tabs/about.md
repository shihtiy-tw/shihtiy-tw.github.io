## About Me

Hi! My name is Shih-Ting, Yuan. I like to simplify my life but I also always complicate things. I'm passionate about discovering new things and engaging in thoughtful discussions.

I'm a Technical Solutions Engineer at Wiz, now part of Google Cloud. Wiz is a security platform for cloud, on-premises, and AI environments. I support customers on the product side, helping them set up, run, and troubleshoot Wiz across cloud connectors, CSPM, shift-left scanning, vulnerability management, and Kubernetes and container security.

Before Wiz, I spent six years at AWS as a Cloud Support Engineer, helping customers with Linux, networking, and container services. I became an Amazon ECS subject matter expert, wrote and updated ECS troubleshooting articles for the AWS Knowledge Center, and made two YouTube videos on ECS and EKS. I wrote about those years in [Lessons from Six Years in AWS Support](/posts/Six-Years-AWS-Cloud-Support-Enginner/).

Outside of work, I am a PADI-certified Advanced Open Water, Enriched Air Nitrox and Dry Suit diver who has explored the underwater wonders in Taiwan and Portugal. I was able to reach the summit of Jade Mountain and go around the island in Taiwan. I also like to dance (Locking style) and enjoy party dance. I host a podcast show [Restart](https://open.spotify.com/show/7CsVZm7vNHJQsOGYzUeh4B) with my friends. I enjoy nature and I am learning about vermiculture with a little worm bin.

I am a husband and a member of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

## 關於我

嗨！我叫袁士庭。我喜歡簡化我的生活，但我也總是把事情複雜化。我對於發現新事物和參與深入的討論充滿熱情，喜歡與其他人分享和討論。

我目前在 Wiz（現為 Google Cloud 旗下）擔任 Technical Solutions Engineer。Wiz 是一個涵蓋雲端、地端和 AI 環境的安全平台。我負責資安產品的技術支援，協助客戶導入、設定產品，並排除使用上的問題，範圍包括雲端連接器（connector）、CSPM、shift-left 掃描、弱點管理，以及 Kubernetes 和容器安全。

加入 Wiz 之前，我在 AWS 擔任雲端支援工程師六年，協助客戶處理 Linux、網路和容器服務的問題。我成為 Amazon ECS 的 SME，為 AWS 知識中心撰寫和更新 ECS 故障排除文章，也拍了兩支 ECS／EKS 的 YouTube 影片。這六年的心得寫在[我在 AWS 技術支援六年的收穫](/posts/Six-Years-AWS-Cloud-Support-Enginner-zho/)。

工作之餘，我是 PADI 進階開放水域 (AOW) 、高氧空氣 (ENA) 以及乾式潛水衣的認證潛水員。我曾在台灣和葡萄牙潛水。我曾攻頂臺灣玉山和環島。我也喜歡跳舞(Locking style)，享受派對舞。我和我的朋友一起錄製 podcast 節目[重新開機](https://open.spotify.com/show/7CsVZm7vNHJQsOGYzUeh4B)。我熱愛大自然，並正在學習蚯蚓養殖，管理一個小蚯蚓箱。

我是一個丈夫，也是耶穌基督後期聖徒教會的成員。
